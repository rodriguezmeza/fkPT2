Scientific references
=====================

Match the references to the observable and gravity model used in your analysis.

Fourier-space power spectrum
----------------------------

* Rodriguez-Meza et al. (2024), *fkPT: Constraining scale-dependent modified
  gravity with the full-shape galaxy power spectrum*. The scale-dependent
  growth approximation and the reference fkPT solver.
  `arXiv:2312.10510 <https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10510>`_.

* Noriega, Aviles, Fromenteau, and Vargas-Magaña (2022), *Fast computation
  of non-linear power spectrum in cosmologies with massive neutrinos*.
  The cold dark matter plus baryon power spectra and the FOLPS-nu computation.
  `arXiv:2208.02791 <https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02791>`_.

Lagrangian kernels and correlations
-----------------------------------

* Aviles and Cervantes-Cota (2017), *Lagrangian perturbation theory for
  modified gravity*. `arXiv:1705.10719 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10719>`_.
* Carlson, Reid, and White (2013), *Convolution Lagrangian perturbation
  theory for biased tracers*. `arXiv:1209.0780 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.0780>`_.
* Aviles et al. (2018), *Nonlinear evolution of initially biased tracers in
  modified gravity*. The CLPT and MGPT conventions for the gravity models
  and real-space correlations.
  `arXiv:1809.07713 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07713>`_.
* Aviles (2018), *Renormalization of Lagrangian bias via spectral parameters*.
  `arXiv:1805.05304 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05304>`_.

Gaussian streaming and EFT
--------------------------

* Wang, Reid, and White (2014), *An analytic model for redshift-space
  distortions*. `arXiv:1306.1804 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1804>`_.
* Vlah, White, and Aviles (2015), *A Lagrangian effective field theory*.
  `arXiv:1506.05264 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05264>`_.
* Vlah, Castorina, and White (2016), *The Gaussian streaming model and
  Convolution Lagrangian effective field theory*.
  `arXiv:1609.02908 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02908>`_.
* Valogiannis, Bean, and Aviles (2020), *Modeling the imprints of modified
  gravity on anisotropic galaxy clustering*.
  `arXiv:1909.05261 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05261>`_.
